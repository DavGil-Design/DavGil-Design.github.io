Flypass · Fuel & Charge · 2026

Charging an EV, as simple as paying a toll

Flypass already handled tolls, parking and fuel for drivers in Colombia. The challenge was adding EV charging: a new service with third-party hardware, variable pricing and many things that can fail in the physical world.

Role
UX Designer end-to-end
Platform
App iOS / Android
Scope
100+ screens and states
Context
Fuel & Charge program (B2B + B2C)

The challenge

Charging an EV in Colombia still raises doubts: does the station work? Is the connector compatible? How much will I pay? What happens if I leave while it's charging? Every doubt is a drop-off.

  • Variable pricing: you pay for energy (kWh), plus post-charge parking and a service fee.
  • Third-party hardware: the charger can be busy, fail, or not detect the cable in time.
  • Long sessions: users don't stare at the app for 30 minutes.

Key decisions

Transparency before charging

Pricing is broken down on the station detail and again during setup. The message is explicit: "you're charged at the end, based on what you use; nothing is charged now". Users decide with full information.

Users set the limit

Users can charge to 80%, 100% or set their own limit in pesos or kWh, with range validation. They control spending without doing math.

Tracking outside the app

I designed iOS Live Activities to follow the charge from the lock screen, with rules for each goal type (percentage, pesos or kWh) and push notifications at every milestone: start, progress, payment and close.

A visual system for the map

Designing for failure

A big part of the work was edge states: network error, cable not connected in time, busy charger, charger failure, no signal while charging, outstanding balance and payment retry. Each one says what happened, whether anything was charged and what to do next.

Outcomes

On the program's B2B side I also designed the fuel management portal for companies: available stations in list and map views, a detail drawer and a station comparator.
