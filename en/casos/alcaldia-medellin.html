i4Digital · Medellín City Hall · 2021—2022

From six weeks to three days: redesigning a public process

Two internal processes at Medellín City Hall relied on paper, emails and manual file searches. With i4Digital we digitized them, starting from how the people who ran them actually worked.

Role
UI/UX Designer
Client
Medellín City Hall
Methods
Contextual research, journeys
Deliverables
MVP, design system, handoff

Outcomes

6 wks → 2-3 daysprocess duration
~2 days → minutesfile and record retrieval
2key processes digitized

Process

Contextual research→Current journey→Remove steps→Prototype→Usability→MVP
  • Contextual research with civil servants to map the real journey of the process, not the one in the manual.
  • Journeys and removal of redundant steps: steps that added no value and manual re-entry that caused delays and errors.
  • Prototypes validated with internal users and clear acceptance criteria to deliver the MVP without sacrificing technical stability.
  • Alignment workshops across product, technology and operations, with documented decisions.

Design system

I co-created a design system with naming conventions and contribution guidelines so several teams could stay consistent. I also mentored a designer and a developer on UX and CSS best practices.
